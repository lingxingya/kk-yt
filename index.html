Douyin → YouTube Pipeline

A personal automation tool for content creators.

What this app does

This is a private, single-user tool that helps its owner move short-form video content they have the right to use from Douyin (the Chinese short-video platform) to their own YouTube channel. It parses a video link, downloads the file, generates an English title, and uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There is no public sign-up, no multi-user support, and no third-party access. The application is not distributed and is not available to the general public.

Google account access

The app requests a single OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload

This scope is used only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, modify, or delete other content, and does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google service.

Data handling

  • Video files are stored temporarily on the owner's own server during processing.
  • Files are deleted automatically immediately after upload completes.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's server and never transmitted to third parties.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
